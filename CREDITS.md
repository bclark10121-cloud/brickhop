## Made for this game

- The Goggles hero: Gemini concept and views, Meshy 3D model and motions, rendered to sprites by `art_src/render_hero/`.
- Blocks, walker and meadow: Gemini pictures in the game's style thread, cut into sprites.
- Menu click and talk blip made by `tools/make_starter_audio.py`.
