# Brickhop v0.2.0

The hero is in. Your Goggles pick from Gemini and Meshy now runs, jumps and stomps through 1-1.

## Five minute test

1. The start screen shows the Goggles hero bouncing between the brick rows.
2. Press Play and stand still. He breathes and shifts in his idle pose.
3. Run right. His legs and arms swing with the run. Hold Run and the steps speed up.
4. Jump. He shows a jump pose going up and a fall pose coming down.
5. Walk into a walker. He shows the hit pose and blinks.
6. Run left. He turns to face left.

## New in this version

- The Goggles hero (BC's pick, hero C) built with Gemini views and a Meshy 3D model, rigged with Idle, Running, Regular Jump and Hit Reaction.
- The model is rendered side-on into a 17-frame sprite sheet: idle (6), run (8), jump, fall and hit.
- The start screen hero is the same character.

## Shared kit

Built with kit 1.16.0. Godot 4.7.2.

## Install

Installs over 0.1.0 and keeps your saves.

## Full test checklist

1. The hero stands on the ground with his boots on the grass, not floating or sunk in.
2. The run cycle loops with no jump or pop.
3. Turned upright, the hero still reads clearly.
4. The bot reaches the flag on Breezy, Standard and Classic (HOP check).

## Not tested

Real phones, the Retroid, a real Xbox controller and touch by hand. Blocks, walkers and sound are still stand-ins.
