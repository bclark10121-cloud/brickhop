# Brickhop v0.4.1

Smooth jumps. Fixes the skipped frame at the top of the jump.

## Five minute test

1. Run and jump on your phone. The hero glides through the top of the jump with no hitch.
2. Tap jump for a short hop. The hop slows and turns around smoothly instead of stopping in mid air.
3. Hold jump for a full jump. It goes as high as before.
4. Stomp a beetle without holding jump. The bounce is as high as before.
5. Run along the ground. The hero and the camera move smoothly with no shudder.

## What was wrong

- Phones with 90 or 120 Hz screens draw more frames than the game's 60 physics steps a second. On some screen frames the hero did not move, then jumped two steps on the next. It showed most at the top of a jump, where the hero moves slowest.
- Letting go of jump cut the climb speed in one step, which looked like a skipped frame.
- The hero switched from the jump pose to the fall pose right at the top.

## Fixed

- The hero and the camera are drawn between physics steps, so every screen frame shows smooth movement.
- A released jump now slows with stronger gravity over a few frames. Full jumps and stomp bounces are unchanged.
- One air pose through the top of the jump. The fall pose shows only once the hero is clearly falling.
- New FEEL check: at 120 frames a second the hero moves on every frame and the climb never changes speed in one jump.
