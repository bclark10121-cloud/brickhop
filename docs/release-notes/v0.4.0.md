# Brickhop v0.4.0

Foes and sounds. Two new toy foes, real sounds and spinning toy coins.

## Five minute test

1. Jump. You hear a springy jump sound. Bump a block and grab a coin: each has its own sound.
2. Coins spin as you run past. The star coins are toy gold coins with an orange star.
3. Past the down pipe, a teal pill bug walks toward you. Stomp it. It curls into a ball.
4. Walk into the ball. It rolls away fast, bounces off walls and knocks out the beetle in its path.
5. Further on, a green spring frog sits, then hops toward you. Stomp it from above.
6. Touch the flag. A three-note jingle plays.

## New in this version

- Pill bug foe: walks like the beetle. Stomp it and it curls into a ball. Kick the ball to knock out other foes for a coin each. On Standard and Classic a rolling ball stings if it hits you. On Breezy it never does.
- Spring frog foe: hops toward you every couple of seconds. It hops more often on Classic.
- New achievement "Strike!": knock out a foe with a kicked ball. It takes the place of "Jingle" (50 coins). "Piggy Bank" (100 coins) stays.
- Sounds from Kenney (CC0, via Patch.gg): jump, coin, Sprout and star coin, stomp, block bump, hurt, pipe, flag and kick.
- Spinning toy coins and a toy star coin from the Gemini style thread.

## Shared kit

Built with kit 1.16.0. Godot 4.7.2.

## Install

Installs over 0.3.0 and keeps your saves.

## Full test checklist

1. FOES check: stomp makes a ball, a walk-in kicks it, the ball knocks out a beetle, the frog hops toward the hero.
2. HOP check: the bot reaches the flag on Breezy, Standard and Classic.
3. Sound slider in Settings changes every new sound.
4. The ball falls into a pit and is gone.

## Not tested

Real phones, the Retroid, a real Xbox controller and touch by hand. The new sounds were picked by name and not heard by a person yet.
