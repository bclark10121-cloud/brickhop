# Brickhop v0.5.0

A floating touch stick and a jump that lands where you expect.

## Five minute test

1. On your phone, put your thumb anywhere on the left half of the screen and slide right. A stick appears under your thumb and the hero runs. Lift your thumb and the stick is gone, so nothing covers the hero.
2. Slide left and right without lifting your thumb. The hero turns at once. The view does not swing.
3. Jump over a few gaps. The hero lands at the same place on the screen every time. The screen does not bob up and down.
4. Tap Jump for a short hop, then hold it for a full jump. Letting go stops the climb quickly. The top of the jump no longer floats.
5. Jump up into the edge of a coin block. If only a sliver of your head hits the edge, you slide past it instead of stopping.

## What changed

Touch controls
- The left and right buttons and the Down button are gone.
- A stick floats under your thumb anywhere on the left half of the screen. It shows only while your thumb is down.
- Slide left or right to move. Slide down on a pipe to go down it. Jump and Run stay at the bottom right.
- A quick tap on Jump always counts.

Camera
- No smoothing and no swing when you turn. The hero pushes the view forward, like the classic games.
- In landscape the view stays level. No up and down bob on every jump.

Jumps and landings
- Letting go of Jump cuts the climb within about 4 frames. No float at the top. A heavier fall.
- A full jump reaches about 4.6 blocks from a walk and about 5.1 at a full run. Height grows smoothly with speed (there was a sudden 0.85 block step).
- Running jumps go less far: about 6.9 blocks at full run (was about 8.2). The bot still clears every gap in 1-1 on all three difficulties.
- A stomp bounce works like a jump. Hold Jump to go higher, let go to cut it.
- Letting go of Run in the air keeps your speed.
- Corner correction: a head that clips a block edge by up to 6 pixels slides past it.
- A hidden block found from below now pushes the hero down. Before, the hero popped up on top of it.
- A stomp right at the top of a jump counts as a stomp, not a hit.

Smoothness
- Foes are drawn between physics steps, like the hero, so they no longer skip on 90 and 120 Hz screens.
- The hero's pose and position change on the same frame when landing.
- The screen shakes only when you get hit, not on every brick bump or stomp.

Also
- Full screen by default in the browser (kit 1.17.0, G5.11).

## Shared kit

Built with kit 1.17.0. Godot 4.7.2.

## Install

Installs over 0.4.2 and keeps your saves.

## Full test checklist

1. TOUCH check (new): the stick starts under a thumb on the left half only, has a dead zone, turns quickly, counts down only for a mostly down push, works with Jump at the same time and disappears when let go.
2. LAND check (new): a hidden block pushes the hero down, a 3 px edge clip slides past, no height step between walk and run speed, the same landing spot on screen across jumps, a quick turn does not swing the camera. It fails on 0.4.2, so it proves the fixes.
3. FEEL check at 60, 90 and 120 frames a second. The gravity limit is now counted per physics step, since the release cut eases in over about 4 steps.
4. HOP check on Breezy, Standard and Classic (the bot runs 1-1 to the flag). FOES check. DAYONE, COMMON, SECRETS and SAVESTORE checks.
5. Browser: the pretend Xbox controller test (title, slot, play, Menu, upright) and a new touch test that drags the stick right, left and lets go.

## Not tested

Your real phone. The stick size and the share of the screen that starts it are guesses until you try them.
