# Brickhop v0.4.2

Higher jumps, built for 120 frames a second.

## Five minute test

1. On your 120 Hz phone, open Settings, then Tester tools, then Screen, then Show frame rate. The corner should read close to 120 fps.
2. Hold jump. The hero goes about 4.5 blocks high, 5 with a running start (was about 3).
3. Watch the top of the jump. The hero slows, hangs a moment and comes down with no hitch.
4. Tap jump for a short hop. It slows smoothly and turns over.
5. Stomp a beetle. The bounce is a little higher than before.

## What changed

- The game moves the hero 120 times a second, to match your phone. Screens at 60 or 90 Hz still draw smoothly between steps.
- Gravity eases between rising, the hang at the top and falling, so the jump never changes pace in one frame. The short-hop cut and the hang now fade in over a tenth of a second.
- Jumps are higher: 650 (was 560) and 710 with a run (was 610). Stomp bounce 470 (was 420).
- Tester tools: Show frame rate.
- This is the first game on the new standard G6.20: smooth at up to 120 frames a second.

## Shared kit

Built with kit 1.16.0. Godot 4.7.2.

## Install

Installs over 0.4.1 and keeps your saves.

## Full test checklist

1. FEEL check at 60, 90 and 120 frames a second: the hero moves on every frame, no pace change in one step, a full jump reaches 4 blocks.
2. HOP check on Breezy, Standard and Classic. FOES check.

## Not tested

Your real phone. Whether Android draws this game at 120 depends on the phone's display settings: the frame rate readout shows it.
