Brickhop

Run, hop and find every secret

Run, jump and stomp. Nothing is ever lost.

Brickhop is a side-scrolling platformer. Run right, hop the gaps, bump blocks for coins and stomp the walkers. A hit or a fall only costs a few coins. Every level hides a secret pipe, a hidden block and three star coins.

What kind of game is this?

Tight jumps. Hold to jump higher. Jumps forgive a press that comes a little early or late.
Soft fail. No lives and no game over. A hit or a fall costs a few coins, never the level.
Secrets. Hidden blocks, a secret pipe and three star coins in every level.
Gray box test. This early build uses plain shapes. The toy art comes next.

Things to do

  • Run 1-1 Toybox Meadow from the start to the flag and beat your best time.
  • Bump coin blocks and find the hidden block.
  • Stomp walkers. Find the Sprout block that soaks up one hit.
  • Press down on the pipe with arrows to reach the secret room.
  • Find all three star coins.
  • Pick Breezy, Standard or Classic in Settings.

Controls

Keyboard
Arrows or A and D move. Space, W, Up or Z jumps (hold for higher). Shift or X runs. Down or S goes down a pipe. Esc opens the Menu.
Xbox controller
Left stick or D-pad moves. A jumps (hold for higher). X or B runs. Down goes down a pipe. Menu or View opens the Menu. In menus A chooses and B goes back.
Touch
Left and right buttons at the bottom left. Jump and Run at the bottom right, Down above them. Menu at the top right.

Play with an Xbox controller

Any Xbox Wireless Controller with Bluetooth works. That is every Series X|S controller and the Xbox One controllers made since 2016. If the plastic around the Xbox button matches the face of the controller, it has Bluetooth. Other controllers that your phone or browser sees as a standard gamepad use the same buttons.

On Android
  1. Hold the Xbox button to turn the controller on.
  2. Hold the small Pair button on top for 3 seconds, until the Xbox button flashes fast.
  3. On your phone, open Settings, then Connected devices, then Pair new device.
  4. Tap Xbox Wireless Controller. The Xbox button stays lit when it is connected.
  5. Open Brickhop. The touch buttons hide as soon as you use the controller.
In your browser
  1. Connect the controller to your computer with a USB cable or Bluetooth.
  2. Open Play in your browser. Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari all work.
  3. Press any button once after the game loads, so the browser sees the controller.
Menus
The D-pad or left stick moves between buttons. A chooses and B goes back. You can play the whole game with only the controller.

Install on Android

  1. Tap Download for Android on this page.
  2. Open the file. Allow installs from your browser if your phone asks.
  3. Tap Install, then Open.

Android may show a Play Protect notice for apps from outside the Play Store. Version 0.1.0 installs over the last version and keeps your saves.
